# Phase 53 go/no-go evidence

Real-money enablement remains blocked. Required evidence includes sustained real observation
windows, zero broker-write audit, deterministic replay, reconciliation continuity, restart
recovery, independent validation, explicit human authorization, and a separately certified
secure execution gateway. Passing software tests, research candidates, paper fills, or shadow
results alone are not authorization to trade live.

The Phase 53 dossier (`quantlab phase53 status`) is read-only. It reports the ten required
gates as `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `NOT_TESTED`, and can at most become
`READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`. It cannot arm the execution gateway, change safety settings,
configure credentials, or submit an order. A missing, stale, failed, synthetic, or unverified
artifact remains an explicit no-go condition.
