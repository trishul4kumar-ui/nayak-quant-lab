# Broker Connectivity

`quantlab broker connect` attaches the read-only mock adapter. Stale connections are not healthy. Reconnect does not erase snapshots. Connectivity cannot disable kill switches, change `LIVE_TRADING`, mutate certification, or authorize itself.

Research aliases: `quantlab research broker`, `account-state`, `account-reconciliation`, `broker-health`, `broker-audit`. Paper OMS keeps `quantlab research reconciliation`.
