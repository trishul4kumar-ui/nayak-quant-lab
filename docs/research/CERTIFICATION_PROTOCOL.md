# Certification Protocol

Live-trading certification (`quantlab.release`) is independent of Prompt 23 model-risk certification (`quantlab.certification`).

Critical `FAIL` and critical `NOT_TESTED` block certification. `WARN` is not `PASS`. Independent validation is mandatory (`validator_id` ≠ `owner_id`). Synthetic evidence cannot certify production or restricted-live stages. Failed attempts are retained.
